## 1. Why this code exists

Contributors do real work for real businesses. Clients trust Groibe with their files, their plans and their people. This code says how we expect you to work, and what you can expect from Groibe. It is plain on purpose.

## 2. Who you work for

- Groibe is accountable to the client. You are accountable to Groibe. You do not take work from a client directly, and a client does not hire you through the platform.
- You talk to the client only in the project conversation on the platform. You do not move the conversation to private email, chat or phone, and you do not give the client your personal contact details to continue outside the project. [LAWYER: effect on existing relationships and on work offered after the project.]
- If a client asks you for extra work, tell your delivery manager. Do not agree to it yourself.

## 3. Honest work

- Describe your skills, experience and past work truthfully, in your profile and in applications.
- Do not claim work that is not yours. Do not copy others' work and call it your own. Name your sources and licences.
- Tell us early if you cannot meet a date or a quality bar. Say what is blocking you. Silence is the problem, not the delay.
- Submit work you would be willing to stand behind. Groibe checks it before the client sees it, but that check does not replace your own.

## 4. Client information

1. **Use client information only for the project it was given for.** Do not use it for yourself, for another client, or for your own portfolio, unless the project allows it and the client has consented.
2. **Open files only through the platform.** Do not forward, copy or share client files or messages by email, chat or any other route.
3. **Do not name the client** in your portfolio or proof-of-work text if the client chose to stay anonymous. Groibe shows the client's name only if the client has agreed.
4. **Protect your device.** Keep it locked, updated and with a screen lock. Do not use a shared or public computer for client files.
5. **Do not copy client data to places you do not control.** Do not put it on personal cloud drives, personal email, or messaging apps.
6. **Do not paste client files or data into an outside automated or AI tool, and do not use them to train or tune any model.** [FOUNDER DECISION: public wording conflicts with the rule in `CLAUDE.md`. Option A keeps the words "AI tool". Option B says "any outside online tool or service that stores or learns from what you give it".]
7. **When your seat ends, delete client files** from your devices and accounts, and confirm if we ask. Groibe cannot check your device. You remain bound by this rule after the project. [LAWYER: period, wording, exceptions for your own submitted work.]
8. **Report problems at once.** If you lose a device, share a file by mistake or think someone saw client data, write to [INCIDENT EMAIL] within [HOURS SET BY LAWYER] and say what happened. We will not punish a quick honest report.

## 5. Personal data of other people

Client files may hold personal data about people who are not you. Treat it as private. Do not collect more than the work needs. Do not look for or keep data about people beyond the task. [LAWYER: Groibe's duties under the Act and the Rules, and how they pass to contributors.]

## 6. Respectful, fair working

- Treat clients, Groibe staff and other contributors with respect. No harassment, discrimination or abuse, in writing or in speech.
- Respect other people's time. Work in the hours and time zones agreed for the project. Do not expect replies outside agreed hours. [LAWYER / FOUNDER: agreed hours, response times.]
- Give credit to others for their work.
- Tell us if you have a conflict of interest, for example a link to the client or to a competitor.
- Do not accept gifts or payments from a client or a third party for work on a project. [LAWYER: define "gift".]
